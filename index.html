About the Book

A story about legacy, survival, and the fight to hold onto what matters most.

When Callie Rigby returns to her family's struggling West Texas ranch, she is met with more than memories; she inherits debt, responsibility, and a ticking clock. With only thirty days to save the land her father built, she must navigate a world far removed from the city life she once knew.

As corporate interests threaten to take over the land, Callie finds herself at odds with Boone West, a man deeply rooted in tradition and wary of change. Their clashing perspectives mirror a larger battle — progress versus preservation, profit versus purpose.

About the Author

A legacy carried forward through story, memory, and promise.

Bessie Mae is more than a name on a book cover; it is a legacy carried forward through story, memory, and promise. Behind this name is a writer who did not set out simply to tell a tale, but to honor a life defined by resilience, sacrifice, and unwavering devotion.

This book was born from a promise — one made in the quiet weight of time, when words matter most. That promise was to share a gift, to give voice to stories that reflect the strength found in hardship and the grace that grows from perseverance.
